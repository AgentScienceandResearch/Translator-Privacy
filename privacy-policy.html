Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 9, 2026

Everyday Translate ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our translation services:

  • Sign in with Apple: If you choose to sign in, we receive your Apple User ID and optionally your name and email (if you choose to share them). This information is stored locally on your device only.
  • Voice Data: When you use voice translation, your speech is processed to provide translations. Audio is not stored permanently.
  • Images: When you use camera translation, images are processed to extract and translate text. Images are not stored permanently.
  • Usage Data: We track the number of translations for the free trial feature. This count is stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide translation services
  • To manage your subscription status
  • To save your language preferences locally

Data Storage

All user preferences and account information are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms. We do not maintain user accounts on external servers.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services to provide translations:

  • OpenAI: For text translation processing
  • ElevenLabs: For natural voice synthesis

These services process your translation requests but do not receive any personal identifying information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Translation content is sent to our processing services solely to provide the translation functionality.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Sign out to clear your account information
  • Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple's App Store settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@everydaytranslate.app

Subscriptions

Everyday Translate offers auto-renewable subscriptions:

  • Weekly subscription: $2.99 per week
  • Monthly subscription: $4.99 per month
  • Yearly subscription: $29.99 per year

Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your subscriptions by going to your App Store account settings after purchase.
