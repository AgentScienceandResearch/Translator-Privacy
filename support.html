Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I restore my subscription?

Go to Settings → Manage Subscription → Tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Select Everyday Translate
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
Q: Voice translation isn't working

Make sure:

  • You have an active subscription
  • Microphone permission is enabled in Settings → Everyday Translate
  • You have a stable internet connection
Q: Camera translation isn't working

Make sure:

  • You have an active subscription
  • Camera permission is enabled in Settings → Everyday Translate
  • The image has clear, readable text
Q: How many free translations do I get?

You get 3 free translations to try the app. After that, you'll need a subscription for unlimited access.

Q: What languages are supported?

We support 40+ languages including:

  • Spanish, French, German, Italian
  • Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean
  • And many more!
Q: Is my data private?

Yes! We don't store your translations or personal data. View our Privacy Policy for details.

Q: Subscription not loading

If subscriptions show "temporarily unavailable":

  • Check your internet connection
  • Make sure you're signed in to the App Store
  • Try restarting the app
  • If the issue persists, contact support
